# HUSH demo route

Start `npm run desktop` for the native integration demo; `npm run dev` is the complete browser game with neutral or explicitly simulated sensing. Use the status table in `INTEGRATION_STATUS.md` before claiming any live service.

Prepare before presenting: open Camera & pulse setup in the desktop app, select optional sensing and let baseline finish in steady light. Pulse recording and shared trace require separate consent. Keep a neutral offline route ready. Do not imply a simulated elevation was measured from a person, or try to force a physiological rise.

A concise demo:

1. Explain the physical objective: repair four research stages, broadcast a remote heartbeat, and lead the creature back into containment.
2. Click the physical computer (or press E) to open its terminal. Show a FAULT box and its number. Clear it, then briefly show the proximity detector and selected security feed. The outside view is fictional CCTV; it is separate from the webcam.
3. Wait for the proximity alarm, then press Q promptly, before contact. Once an attack commits at the wall it cannot be interrupted. The machines stop, the creature listens and physically retreats. Point to the equivalent receding-footstep caption. Hold F after the clear window. Research stays saved.
4. Complete the fault stage, watch five memory pulses (six at emitter B), repeat them and press T. The remote signal has a guaranteed protected effect.
5. Open J to show a validated tactical choice, its public event IDs, and exact LIVE/CACHED/FALLBACK labels. Close J and finish the remaining research. The final broadcast requires actual arrival and sealing; it is not a timer win.
6. Show the optional local report. Explain that event timing does not establish causation; gaps and pauses break continuity. Delete the report and consenting session after the demo.

A practiced expert route is roughly two minutes before explanation or pauses. A guided first play and defensive interruptions take longer. Do not promise a fixed duration.
