# /src/ui — Lea (art, audio & UI)

React presentation for HUSH: shelter setup, the pre-rendered opening cutscene (`OpeningFilm.tsx`, which plays `assets/video/opening.mp4` with captions from `src/cutscene/timeline.ts` and falls back to text cards under reduced motion or a playback error; see `cutscene/README.md`; after a win App reuses it for `assets/video/ending.mp4` with the ending's captions, or OUTRO text cards, and the run report follows the film), seven-step guided practice, task HUD, quiet/restore controls, station navigation, captions, pause, judge trace and end-of-run pulse report. Three.js scene lifetime belongs to `src/render`; the deterministic simulation belongs to `src/game`. App adapts authoritative state into those presentation APIs without waiting on provider calls.

- The current runtime ships 10 GLBs, 27 synthesized WAVs, four fictional CCTV PNGs and seven SVGs. `public/assets/manifest.json` contains file paths, sizes and SHA-256 hashes; `docs/ASSETS.md` records source/license/runtime use. Preserve the independent build kits.
- The game is a sealed, windowless shelter with an unseen exterior creature. Tasks silence faults, calibrate five/six-number sequences (four in guided practice) and transmit remote lures. The reinforced door does not open. Remove obsolete hatch, shutter, shift-supervisor or order-verification assumptions from new UI work.
- `1–4` enter task choices, `R` replays the sequence, `T` transmits, `Q` isolates equipment but cannot cancel a committed close-range attack and holding `F` restores when allowed. Keep accessible buttons and keyboard behavior equivalent. Tutorial arrows must identify actual controls without covering the sound caption or emergency inputs.
- Distance, danger and CCTV displays are unavailable while equipment is quiet or blacked out. Puzzle progress persists. Sound captions use the same creature movement/warning state as audio; never reveal hidden exact distance in an audio-only state.
- Personal camera sensing is separate from fictional facility CCTV. Explicit opt-in, neutral manual play, labelled simulation, quality/staleness handling, visible stop controls and local pulse-report consent must remain clear. Camera frames, raw physiology and secrets must not enter shared judge traces.
- Optional provider work stays asynchronous and run-scoped. Validate proposals in the game layer, discard stale responses and cancel obsolete speech. Every spoken line has an exact caption. Live, cached, fallback and simulated labels require actual evidence; no fabricated live success.
- React renders menus and overlays, not a new Three.js scene each frame. On restart/unmount, release listeners, timers, capture, audio, scene and subscriptions; preserve pause semantics.
- Keep near-black corroded steel, sparse amber desk lighting, cream labels and restrained red alarm lighting. Maintain readable captions, keyboard focus, reduced motion, softer impacts, master volume and mute. No full-screen flashes.
- Keys remain in Node-side Vite middleware, the Electron main process or provider backend, never Vite variables or renderer code. Do not send messages, deploy services or make paid provider requests just to improve presentation.
- Check the actual UI at desktop and narrow sizes, including quiet/blackout, tutorial arrows, sequence playback, pause and results. Unit/asset parser success is not rendered-game verification.

- Tasks appear only after clicking actual equipment or pressing E while facing it. Space closes inspection; station navigation only turns the view. Do not restore a permanent task sidebar. Never show an advance breach banner/countdown; accessible captions describe sounds only.
- Camera setup uses the controller-owned stream for its mirrored local preview and device selector. It needs explicit activation and native desktop support. Calibration may run while the game is deliberately paused in its camera dialog; hiding the app stops capture.
- Creature speech follows the exterior creature position and remains eligible during quiet equipment. Exact authored FIRST_APPROACH dialogue is requested on the first alarm, with bounded later taunts and cancellation/expiry checks.
- `JumpScare` breach is an animated attack on the existing scare image: twitching head, a jaw layer that snaps open and chatters, eye glints, colour ghosts, tears and a pulsing vignette, lunging in over 1.6 s and then holding on the face with no fade-out until the run resets. No bright or full-screen flash frames. Loss results appear at `SCARE_MS.breach - 200` (4.4 s) over the held face. Softer scares show no face; reduced motion holds a still frame. `tests/fixtures/jumpscare.html` renders every kind for visual checks.
- `PulseMonitor` never blanks or flags a dropout: between accepted estimates it keeps showing the last accepted pulse/breathing (pulse falls back to the calibrated baseline) at normal brightness, and only the signal dot stops glowing. The memory (`vitalsHold.ts`) is shared by every readout, survives pauses, stale data and new capture segments, and clears only when the source changes. The player never sees their own readings or camera during play (2026-10-25, user decision): App no longer renders `CornerVitals`, and the HUD camera button names only the camera state (CAMERA ON, CALIBRATING, CAMERA UNAVAILABLE; the SIMULATED dev label keeps its trend). The camera check keeps its framing preview but no readouts. Spectators get one FEAR reading instead (`readSpectatorFear`): the player's combined camera fear number when it is shared, otherwise an estimate from heart rate, labelled which. The judge trace keeps the exact LIVE/STALE label. Display only; the game still receives neutral physiology for missing data.
- Run-report charting has no separate switch: it is on whenever pulse input (camera or simulation) is, and enabling the camera is the opt-in, disclosed in the camera prompt and Operator settings. A camera started mid-run records from then on for that run. `PulseReport` names why a report is empty (camera off, or no accepted readings). Delete report & shared session still erases it.
- `CameraPrompt` opens on the first Begin/Skip of a session when pulse input is off: enable camera & set up Presage (the real `CameraPanel`, with start-now or start-calibrated), or ‘I don’t want to turn on my camera’ for neutral play. Browser builds explain the desktop requirement. Browser tests decline it in `begin()`.
- `ThinkingTasks.tsx` renders the by-ear fault panel (plain 1–4 buttons, the sound key while it plays, a YOU HEAR caption; never a FAULT marker), the conduit board, locate (terminal hint plus marking buttons on the security monitor), heartbeat mask and calm hold. The in-room terminal screen never marks the failing machine either. The pulse flash and calm ring are driven by game time on each HUD render; App plays a `terminal_press` tick per beat and maps B (hold/release) to `breathe`. `WirePuzzle` shows one six-cable panel at a time; wires are drawn in board pixels from the measured button edges, so keep each button inside its grid row. On desktop the security-monitor panel docks bottom-right (and the scene view shifts slightly on 4:3 screens) so the in-room CCTV feed stays visible for locate. `tests/fixtures/tasks.html#<step>` renders each panel from real reducer states.
- Co-op manual (`CoopManual.tsx`) is a book the friend flips through, printed from the run's seed (`coopBook`): contents plus eight pages (rack sounds, cable codes, conduit boards, signal symbols, radar keys, live breathing pacer, heartbeat mask, door key), tabs, ←/→ page turns and grey static bursts while the creature is close (steady under reduced motion). In co-op the player's sockets show book codes (`WirePuzzle` `codes`), memory playback shows glyphs and the locate keys are neutral. The HUD voice button shows a whisper meter and TOO LOUD when the game hears the player's mic.
- The spectate page (`SpectateView.tsx`, worn two-column layout) adds sound and an opt-in browser-voice narrator, an outside camera under the shelter camera, a reaction spotlight on scares, a pulse line under FEAR, highlights, predictions and an end-of-run recap; after a win the researcher walks out through the basement door. See docs/SPECTATE.md. Spectators cannot change the player's run.
- Guided practice is voiced (2026-10-25): each card's text is its `TUTORIAL_*` line in `src/game/dialogue.json` (`tutorialLineId`), read once in the radio voice the first time the card shows (at least 1.6 s apart for the provider rate limit), cancelled when the card changes, with no second radio caption. Friend-run controls (LIVE, MIC, Stop sharing) sit in `.share-controls` under sound/pause at the top right; the footer is a three-column grid above 720 px so nothing lands on the workstation buttons. Opening-film captions describe sounds only.
