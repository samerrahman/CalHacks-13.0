THE EXPERIENCE
A researcher escapes a containment failure into a small reinforced, windowless shelter in a forest research facility. Only its interior needs full 3D rendering. A brief dark-screen opening uses alarm, running, a door slam and captions, followed by a damaged recording explaining remote containment. Make the opening skippable and concise. The creature stays outside; the player learns its location through position-driven footsteps, scraping, breathing, a proximity unit, fictional security feeds and occasional voice. There are no player-operated shutters, corridor defense lanes, identity-code radio puzzles, floodlight sweeps, battery camping or timer-only wins.

The research protocol is the objective:
1. Silence five faults across four machines.
2. Calibrate a four-input sequence and transmit to remote emitter A.
3. Clear seven overload faults, with up to two active at once.
4. Calibrate a second four-input sequence and transmit to emitter B.
Then enable the final containment broadcast. The creature must physically follow its route into containment before the gate seals and the game declares victory. Waiting, elapsed time or maintaining a pulse target cannot win.

A breach is a loss only after the deterministic creature approaches, investigates, earns sufficient nonphysiological sound evidence and completes a visible/audible learned warning. The door impact/dent and cut to black communicate death without a full interior monster reveal. The player always has a fair escape response until the actual breach boundary.

GAMEPLAY CONTRACTS
Use the supplied config for starting tuning and centralize changes there. Maintain these invariants when playtesting:
- One creature, one local authority, fixed-step deterministic state given seed/time/inputs. The external facility is a small logical route graph, not an open world.
- Proximity, distance bands, camera visibility, directional sound, wall impacts and captions derive from that same position/route/state. No unrelated random danger meter or fabricated nearby footsteps.
- Active faults create continuous sound evidence. Quiet removes new machinery evidence, but existing location confidence decays over time. Mistakes and completion/startup sounds are finite authored clues with explicit strength/expiry. A successful task grants its concrete benefit.
- Fault buttons show both a visible number and FAULT/QUIET. A quiet-box click clangs; correct faults count reliably. Completion is never conditional on physiology. Completed stages persist.
- Memory has exactly four inputs and four buttons. Code selects the destination card; it is not a second guessing puzzle. R replays the same sequence freely. Capture tempo at presentation start; sustained elevation may speed playback by at most the configured 1.2 multiplier, never change it midway or tighten input deadlines. Wrong input produces one bounded error clue and restarts the current sequence, not all research.
- Q quiet works immediately from any station, beginning, blackout and warning. It silences all noisy equipment and freezes puzzle work. Follow the authored listening then physical retreat response; do not teleport or instantly erase memory. Pulse cannot negate this escape.
- After the correct receding cue, holding F for one second restores equipment and earns the configured safe work period. Provide a click-based accessible equivalent. Early restart creates its startup clue and does not earn an unearned safety reward.
- Successful A/B lures reliably redirect the creature, including a warning already in progress. Protect at least 16 seconds plus required physical routing/arrival hold; local noise, pulse, AI and blackout cannot cancel the reward. A fresh approach/warning is required after it ends. Final broadcast remains protected until containment succeeds.
- Sedation wears off at the configured elapsed game times; movement/search pressure ramps to a bounded maximum, while approach minima, warning minima, recovery and encounter budgets remain intact. No hidden instant lethal phase or unavoidable starvation timer.
- Blackouts occur only at eligible moments with cooldown/maximum. Lights, terminal, security feed, distance and danger displays go out. Puzzle sequence/progress/cursor remain saved; task clock pauses, creature/sedation continue. Q/F and the illuminated switch remain available. Footstep direction/recession and equivalent accessible captions convey when to restart. Never schedule an unavoidable warning during forced input loss.
- Pause freezes all game timers and stops capture/audio. Restart/menu/unmount dispose listeners, animation loops, cameras, timers and pending callback effects. Async results from old runs cannot act on a new seed.
